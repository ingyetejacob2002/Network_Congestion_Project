INTERPRETATION
Use timestamp-grouped validation as the more cautious estimate when repeated records come from the same timestamp. Row-wise CV can be optimistic if closely related records are spread across folds.
A difference between row-wise and grouped scores indicates sensitivity to the validation strategy. Neither strategy proves real-world congestion detection because the target labels were assigned from source scenarios.
Do not select a final model solely from accuracy. Review macro-F1, per-class reports, confusion matrices, timestamp ranges, and how the labels were created.
