INTERPRETATION NOTES:
The Kolmogorov-Smirnov test compares empirical distributions. A small p-value indicates evidence of a difference, but does not by itself establish that the difference is practically important or that the model is invalid.
The Kruskal-Wallis test checks whether distributions differ across classes. Statistical significance alone does not prove that a feature is useful for prediction.
Repeated feature vectors can arise naturally from simulated traffic, counters, or rounded measurements. They should be interpreted in the context of how the data was generated.
